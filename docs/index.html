What is associated with leaving? (all employees)

Rate ratios from a Poisson regression with person-years exposure. 1.30 means a 30% higher exit rate with other factors held equal; below 1 means lower. Departments are compared with Engineering, tenure bands with 5+ years.

Caveats

  • The data is synthetic, so the patterns demonstrate the analysis rather than report real findings.
  • Associations are not proof of cause. Pay, workload and tenure are related, and manager quality or personal reasons are not measured.
  • Voluntary and involuntary exits are not separated.
  • Only hires from 2023 onward are used for early-tenure measures, because earlier leavers are absent from the data.
  • Small groups have wide uncertainty - check the confidence intervals in the tooltips.
  • Privacy: only an anonymous employee ID is stored; no names, ages, gender or contact details.
